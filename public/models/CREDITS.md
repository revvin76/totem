## Additional bundled geometry

- `assets/models/snes-cad.glb`: Super Nintendo geometry from [Open Console CAD](https://github.com/tiansongyu/open-console-cad) by tiansongyu, MIT. Converted to GLB with Totem's Y-up showcase orientation and baked assembly transforms.
- `assets/models/flatpanel-tv.glb`: [Television](https://grab3d.com/models/electronics/television/) by Grab3D, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Totem uses the separate screen mesh for an optional video texture and can replace the cabinet in Studio.
- `games/models/templates/*.glb`: generic cases and cartridges generated by Totem from the project template definitions. No game labels or cover textures are baked into these meshes. These templates are supplied for use with Totem. They do not include third-party game artwork.
